How to read the results

  • Outperform is the share of all rolling windows in which the fund beat its benchmark's CAGR over the same months.
  • Upside and Downside split that by whether the benchmark rose or fell in the window. A fund can beat rarely overall yet protect well on the downside; read them separately.
  • Excess is the latest window's fund CAGR minus benchmark CAGR, not an average.
  • Excess-return mode applies the workbook's fourth screen: fund CAGR above benchmark and fund volatility below benchmark in the same window.

Basis: month-end NAVs from April 2006, monthly rolls, TRI benchmarks. A scheme needs about six years of history before any of this is computable. Consistency is a description of the past; it is not a probability of future outperformance.
